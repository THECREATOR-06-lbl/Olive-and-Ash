ABOUT US

Rustic food, open flame, no rush.

Olive & Ash opened with one idea: cook the way the season tells you to, over real wood, and let people linger. Our kitchen leans on Italian technique and West African produce, and our wine list is small, natural, and chosen by hand.

“Wood smoke, good wine, and nowhere you need to be.”
